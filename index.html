About the Institution

Education, formation and a vocation to serve.

St. Kizito Minor Seminary is a Catholic educational and formation institution in the Diocese of Dedza. The seminary was established in 1968 and is located in Malirana, Dedza.

A legacy of formation

The seminary has contributed to the formation of priests and leaders in the Church and wider society. This digital platform is designed to bring the institution's academic and community services into one modern environment.
