Our identity

A tradition of education, formation and service.

St. Kizito Minor Seminary opened its doors in January 1968 after a vision associated with the late Bishop Cornelius Chitsulo. Over the decades, the institution has contributed to the formation of priests, Church leaders and professionals serving Malawi and beyond.

Its historic motto, “Friends we are on the way,” expresses a journey of growth, friendship, discernment and formation.

Seminary formation

A seminary is more than a classroom. The formation journey develops the whole person through spiritual, human, intellectual and pastoral dimensions.

1963

The vision begins

The late Bishop Cornelius Chitsulo developed the vision of establishing a minor seminary in the Diocese of Dedza.

1968

St. Kizito opens its doors

The seminary opened at the end of January 1968 in Malirana, Dedza.

2018

Golden Jubilee

The institution celebrated fifty years of service to the Church, education and Malawi.
